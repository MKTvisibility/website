Keep your business information consistent

MKT Visibility · October 30, 2026

A holiday schedule changes. Someone updates the website, but the social profile and community page still show regular hours. A customer makes an unnecessary trip. The promotion may have worked perfectly; the information failed at the final step.

Accuracy needs an owner and a process. Treat business details as a shared reference rather than information scattered across old messages.

Create one approved information sheet

Include your public business name, customer-facing contact details, website, service area or visitor address, regular hours, holiday exceptions, booking links, current services, and a short description. Add the date each item was confirmed and the person authorized to approve changes.

Be careful with home addresses and appointment-only locations. Publish only information appropriate to the business and consistent with platform rules. An internal record can contain details that should never appear publicly.

Google advises businesses to represent themselves accurately and follow its profile guidelines. Source: Guidelines for representing your business on Google. Consistency is useful for customers; small formatting differences alone should not be treated as proof of a search-ranking problem.

Use a change checklist

When hours, services, contact details, or offers change, identify every affected destination. Update the approved information sheet, make the changes where authorized, test the public result, and mark the work complete. Include Loving Longmont or MKT Colorado when your listing contains the changed detail.

For an event, confirm the date, start time, location, price, registration requirements, and cancellation information. An attractive image should not carry an incorrect date further across the internet.

How MKT approaches the work

An organized visibility service should gather approved facts at onboarding and establish how owners communicate changes. It should distinguish an item awaiting confirmation from an approved update. Some platforms review edits before displaying them; submission and public appearance are separate steps to check.

MKT can coordinate updates within the agreed scope, but the owner remains the source of truth for operating decisions. Marketing cannot safely infer a holiday closure or service change from an old post.

Try it yourself

Choose the three places customers most often find you. Compare hours, contact details, and the main action link. Record inconsistencies, correct those you control, and request corrections elsewhere. Recheck any change that does not appear immediately.

Where professional help adds value

The value lies in keeping a reliable record and following the changes through several systems. That coordination becomes more important as a business adds locations, seasonal schedules, or events.

Make business-information updates a repeatable task, especially before seasonal promotions.
