Use photography and video to reduce uncertainty

MKT Visibility · October 30, 2026

A photograph of a storefront can do more than make a page attractive. It can show which door to use. A short process video can explain what happens at an appointment. A product image can show scale that a written dimension alone leaves difficult to imagine.

Choose visuals by what they help a customer understand. Attractive content is useful when it also represents the real business accurately.

Plan the shot before you make it

List the questions the visual should answer. For a local studio, that might include the entrance, work area, materials, staff, and finished examples. Capture both wider context and relevant details. Keep the background intentional, avoid exposing private records, and confirm permission before including identifiable customers.

An image found through search is not automatically available for business use. Ownership of a copy does not grant every reproduction right. The Copyright Office explains that photographs and other original expression can be protected. Source: What copyright protects. Keep a record of licenses and permissions for material you publish.

Make the information accessible

Use text alternatives for meaningful website images and avoid repeating unnecessary detail for purely decorative ones. W3C's image tutorial explains how the purpose of an image affects its alternative text. Source: W3C image guidance.

For video with meaningful speech or sound, provide accurate captions. W3C distinguishes captions from other alternatives and explains their role in making audio information available. Source: W3C captions guidance. Review automatic captions, especially business names, prices, and instructions. Include important visual information in narration or an appropriate text alternative when needed.

How MKT approaches the work

A content session should start with a shot list and intended uses. Preparation includes scheduling, location choices, permissions, capture, selection, editing, format adjustments, accessibility checks, and approval. These tasks explain why professional content work involves more than the time spent holding a camera.

Not every post needs a professional shoot. Owner-supplied images can be useful when they are accurate, clear, and authorized. MKT can help identify where existing material is sufficient and where new capture would solve a real information gap.

Try it yourself

Take three photographs that answer three different customer questions. Write a short explanation for each. Check whether someone unfamiliar with the business understands what the picture shows and why it matters.

Where professional help adds value

Professional help creates a reusable, organized set of assets designed for actual marketing needs. The goal is a practical content library that saves repeated last-minute effort.

Choose visuals that explain, orient, or demonstrate something useful about the real business.
