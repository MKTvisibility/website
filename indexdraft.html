FAQ

Before you hand us your marketing.

Do I have to sign up for every service?

No. The packages are starting points. We can focus on the areas where your business needs the most help and quote larger one-time projects separately.

Do you create the content for me?

Yes. Depending on your package, MKT can write copy, create branded graphics, schedule posts and produce original photography or video when included or added to your plan.

Can you fix my website too?

Routine website updates can be included in monthly service. Larger redesigns, new websites and complex development are quoted separately after we review the project.

Do you manage Google Business Profiles?

Yes. We can help keep business information, photos and activity current as part of an ongoing visibility plan.

What about paid ads?

Paid campaigns can be scoped separately. Advertising spend paid to platforms is not included in the monthly package prices shown above.
