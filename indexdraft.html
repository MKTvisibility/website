FAQ

Before you hand us your marketing.

Do I have to sign up for every service?

No. Choose individual one-time or ongoing services based on your priorities. We confirm the scope and total estimate before work begins.

Do you create the content for me?

Yes. Depending on the services you choose, MKT can write copy, create branded graphics, schedule posts and produce original photography or video when included or agreed separately.

Can you fix my website too?

Website SEO and conversion improvements are available as a one-time service for up to five priority pages and calls to action. Larger redesigns, new websites, and other ongoing website support are quoted separately.

Do you manage Google Business Profiles?

Yes. We can help keep business information, photos and activity current as part of an ongoing visibility plan.

Can AI or an automated marketing tool replace MKT Visibility?

AI and automated tools can be useful for creating first drafts, templates and scheduling routine content. But they do not deeply know your business owner, notice a real community story, take authentic photos, build relationships, decide what makes your business special, or make sure every detail is accurate and on-brand. MKT Visibility uses efficient tools where they help, then adds local perspective, real content and human review before your business is represented online.

What about paid ads?

Paid campaigns can be scoped separately. Advertising spend paid to platforms is not included in the monthly service prices. Low-cost marketing subscriptions also do not include your advertising budget, and third-party platform or campaign fees may apply.
